Hi, I'm Jordan.

I'm a full-time CTO (with Run for Something), a consulting fractional CTO (most recently for LingroLearning), and consistently just one post away from being what I'd consider a writer. More importantly, I'm a dad in Minneapolis who loves history, good coffee, and building things on my computer. In a good week, you can catch me exploring all three with my hobby project, Historio.

LET'S SOLVE A PROBLEM TOGETHER

My capacity to take on consulting work is very limited (see above RE kids). That said, I have a hard time saying no to using novel tech to solve the right challenge with the right people.

The right challenges include:

  • How to get the right people elected to public office
  • How to empower local leaders to better serve their communities
  • How to teach kids technical things
  • How to better connect histories, and apply them to the present

The right people include: Collaborative creatives with a clear sense of how their impact will better others.

Tools I Use and Others' Thoughts I Think About

I'm particular about my tools. I like trying new ways of working, experimenting with new workflows, and finding that productivity hack that actually makes me more productive.

My Stack

Notion
For my half-written notes, half-thought-out ideas, and half-completed projects. Which used to be a mess, but now claude makes pretty good sense of it all.
Sunsama
I've tried literally every todo app, and Sunsama is the one that has stuck. I use it to prioritize weekly objectives and daily tasks. Sunsama provides (and kind forces) the right balance of prioritizing vs doing.
Claude & Claude Code
Just trying to be a little more productive before the singularity.
VSCode
More for reading than writing code these days. Still the GOAT.
Pen.dev
Lightweight Figma that claude can control with version controlled files you can save in project repos.
Readwise
To track and sync my notes on Kindle books and articles.
Raycast
A better Mac Spotlight, with focus mode, snippets, and shortcuts for common app actions.
Xbox Game Pass
So I can play Baldur's Gate 3 with my dad.
📓 Dot grid notebooks
Sorry Sunsama, there's truly no better way to lock in on a todo list than a dot grid notebook and pencil (I'll die on the pencil > pen hill).
The Economist
My primary source of news. Huge life improvement when I went from reading tweets every 5 minutes to long-form articles every week. The Intelligence is excellent, too.
